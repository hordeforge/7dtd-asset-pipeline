# Review — <subject>

Named for the subject, not the date. These notes are allowed to be rough, and
allowed to disappear once their conclusion lands somewhere durable.

## What this is

The thing under review — a design, an incident trace, another project's
approach, a diff — and why it was opened.

## Notes

Working observations, in whatever order they arrived. Mark anything unverified
as `unverified` rather than quietly promoting a guess to a finding.

## Conclusion

What it came to, and **where it went**: an [ADR](../adrs/) for a decision, a
[report](../reports/) for a failure with evidence, a [digest](../digests/) for
another project's technique, a [research page](../research/) for an engine
fact, or an entry in [status/improvements.md](../status/improvements.md) for a
gap. A conclusion that stayed here is one nobody will find.
