# Research — engine facts and their provenance

Evidence pages: every claim here was measured against an artifact or a tool,
and names which one. The owning page is
[research-provenance.md](research-provenance.md); new research into engine
behaviour either extends it or starts a page beside it from
[TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md).

- [unityz capability and migration audit](unityz-capability-audit.md) — the
  repository-wide mapping from current Unity-format work to unityz, including
  the exact creation-side gaps that still prevent removing UnityPy.

A fact without a named source (`Data/Config/*.xml`, `ilspycmd`/`monodis` on
`Assembly-CSharp.dll`, or hordeforge/7dtd-engine-research) does not belong in
this directory. "It seemed to work in game" is not a source, and the next
session cannot re-verify it.
