# Working review logs

Notes written *while* reviewing or investigating something — a design, an
incident trace, another project's approach — that have not (yet) hardened into
a [report](../reports/), an [ADR](../adrs/), or a
[digest](../digests/). These are working documents: rough, replaceable, and
allowed to disappear once their conclusion lands somewhere durable.

## Conventions

- Name files after the subject, not the date.
- Say at the top what the notes are about and where the conclusion went when
  it moved.
